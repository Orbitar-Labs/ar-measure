Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · Replaces the version of 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbitar Labs ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the AR Measure app for Android, package name app.armeasure (the "App"). Orbitar Labs decides how and why the information described here is used, so it is the "data controller" (GDPR), the "business" (California law) and the "data fiduciary" (India's DPDP Act) for it. We built the App so that your measurements and photos stay on your device. The only information that leaves your device automatically is technical crash and diagnostic data, which helps us fix bugs.

In short

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts or location.
  • The camera is used only to measure in augmented reality. The live camera image is processed on your device. We never upload or record it.
  • Photos you capture and your settings are stored only on your device.
  • Crash and diagnostic reports, tied to a random installation ID and not to your name, are sent to Firebase Crashlytics (Google).
  • We do not sell or share your data, show ads, or use your data for advertising. The App does not use the advertising ID.

1. Information the App uses on your device

Camera

The App asks for camera permission because augmented-reality measuring needs the live camera image. The image and your device's motion-sensor readings are processed on your device, in real time, by Google Play Services for AR (see section 3) to detect surfaces and calculate distances. The App does not record, upload or keep the camera feed, and it does not use ARCore's cloud features (Cloud Anchors or the Geospatial API). You can withdraw camera permission at any time in your device's settings, but the App cannot measure without it.

Photos you capture

When you tap the capture button, the App saves a photo of the screen, including your measurements, together with its name, the time it was taken and a short measurement summary. This is stored only in the App's private storage on your device. We cannot access it. The App does not add location data to photos and does not need access to your device's photo gallery.

Settings

Your chosen language, default unit and whether you have finished onboarding are stored on your device only.

Sharing

A photo or the App's store link is shared only when you choose Share and pick another app (for example, a messaging or email app). What happens next is governed by that app's privacy policy, not ours.

2. Information collected automatically: crash and diagnostic reports

To find and fix problems, the App uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google LLC. Crashlytics and the Firebase components it depends on (Firebase installations and Firebase sessions) send us:

  • crash details and stack traces (the technical state of the App when it failed), including a short technical note of what the App was doing, for example "saving a photo failed";
  • device information, such as manufacturer and model, Android version, available memory and disk space, screen orientation and the type of network connection;
  • App information, such as the App version, the package name and where the App was installed from;
  • session information: the time the App was opened or brought to the foreground, which is used to work out how many sessions end in a crash;
  • two random identifiers created for your installation of the App: a Crashlytics installation ID and a Firebase installation ID. They are not your advertising ID and are not linked to your name or account, but they do let reports from the same installation be grouped together, so they are personal data under some laws. They are replaced with new ones if you clear the App's data or reinstall the App.

As with any internet connection, Google's servers see your device's IP address when a report is sent. We do not use it to locate or identify you.

Crash reports do not contain your photos, measurements or camera images. We use this data only to diagnose and fix problems and to improve the App's stability. It is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). According to Google, Crashlytics keeps crash stack traces and the identifiers attached to them for 90 days before it starts removing them. The current version of the App does not have a switch to turn crash reporting off; if you do not want this data sent, please do not use the App. You can read more in Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Google Play Services for AR (ARCore)

This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Google Play Services for AR is a separate Google app on your device. Google may collect and process data through it, for example to keep it up to date and working, as described by Google. See how Google Play Services for AR handles your data.

4. The legal pages inside the App

When you open the Privacy Policy or Terms of Use from the App's Settings, the App loads these pages from our website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). As with any website, GitHub receives your device's IP address and basic browser details, and may keep them in its server logs for security. The pages do not use cookies, scripts or analytics of ours. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Links on these pages to other websites, such as Google's, are governed by those websites' policies.

5. How we use and share information, and our legal basis

We use crash and diagnostic data only to keep the App working, to find and fix faults and to improve its stability. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in providing a stable and secure App (Article 6(1)(f)); you can object to this at any time (see section 9). Camera processing and the photos you save happen on your device at your request.

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not give it to third parties for their own marketing. Crash and diagnostic data is processed by Google as our service provider (data processor) under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or if the App is transferred to another company, in which case this policy continues to apply to data already collected.

6. Where data is processed

Orbitar Labs is based in India. Google may process crash and diagnostic data in any country where it or its sub-processors have facilities, including the United States. For transfers of personal data from the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google relies on the safeguards described in the Firebase terms linked above and in Privacy and Security in Firebase, including the Data Privacy Frameworks to which Google LLC has certified.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Photos: stay on your device until you delete them in the App, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.
  • Settings: stay on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.
  • Crash and diagnostic reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days, after which Google starts removing them from its live and backup systems.

The App does not back up your data to the cloud. Because we do not hold your photos or settings, uninstalling the App removes them permanently, and nobody can restore them.

How to ask us to delete data. Email us at the address in section 12 with the subject "Delete my data". Because crash data is not linked to your name or email, please tell us your device model, the App version and the approximate date and time of the crash or of your last use, so that we can look for the matching reports. We will delete what we can identify and reply within 30 days. If we cannot identify your reports, they are deleted automatically at the end of the 90-day period above, and clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App stops any link to future reports.

8. Security

Your photos and settings are kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and are excluded from device backups. Crash and diagnostic data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and the App does not allow unencrypted network traffic. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information.

You can exercise any of the rights below by emailing us at the address in section 12. We will not treat you differently for doing so. Because the App does not collect your name or contact details, we may need the information described in section 7 to find data about you.

California and other US states (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws)

Send grievances to the contact in section 12; we aim to resolve them within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children

The App is a general-purpose measuring tool. It is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age at which the law of your country lets you agree to data processing on your own, which is up to 16 in parts of Europe and 18 in India), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are below that age, please use the App only with a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
