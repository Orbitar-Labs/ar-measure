Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbitar Labs ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the AR Measure app for Android (the "App"). We built the App so that your measurements and photos stay on your device. The only information that leaves your device automatically is technical crash data, which helps us fix bugs.

In short

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts or location.
  • The camera is used only to measure in augmented reality. We never upload, record or store the live camera feed.
  • Photos you capture and your settings are stored only on your device.
  • If the App crashes, anonymous technical crash reports are sent to Firebase Crashlytics (Google).
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your data for advertising.

1. Information the App uses

Camera

The App asks for camera permission because augmented-reality measuring needs the live camera image. The image is processed on your device, in real time, to detect surfaces and calculate distances. We do not record, upload or keep the camera feed. You can withdraw camera permission at any time in your device's settings, but the App cannot measure without it.

Photos you capture

When you tap the capture button, the App saves a photo of the screen, including your measurements, together with its name, the time it was taken and a short measurement summary. This is stored only in the App's private storage on your device. We cannot access it. The App does not add location data to photos.

Settings

Your chosen language, default unit and whether you have finished onboarding are stored on your device only.

Sharing

A photo or the App's store link is shared only when you choose Share and pick another app (for example, a messaging or email app). What happens next is governed by that app's privacy policy, not ours.

2. Information collected automatically: crash reports

To find and fix problems, the App uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google LLC. If the App crashes or a serious error occurs, Crashlytics collects:

  • crash details and stack traces (the technical state of the App when it failed);
  • device information, such as device model, Android version, available memory and disk space, and screen orientation;
  • App information, such as the App version and the time of the crash;
  • a Crashlytics installation identifier: a random ID created for your installation of the App, which is not linked to your identity and changes if you reinstall the App.

Crash reports do not contain your photos, measurements or camera images. We use them only to diagnose and fix problems and to improve the App's stability. According to Google, Crashlytics keeps crash stack traces and their identifiers for 90 days. Data is encrypted in transit. You can read more in Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Google Play Services for AR (ARCore)

The App runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy. ARCore may collect and process data as described by Google. See ARCore privacy requirements for details.

4. How we use and share information

We use crash data only to keep the App working. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. Crash data is processed by Google as our service provider, as described above. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Photos: stay on your device until you delete them in the App, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.
  • Settings: stay on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days, then deleted.

The App does not back up your data to the cloud. Because we do not hold your photos or settings, uninstalling the App removes them permanently. To ask us to delete crash data linked to your installation, contact us (see below).

6. Security

Your photos and settings are kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Crash data is sent over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU/UK GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to withdraw consent, and to raise a grievance. Because the App does not collect your name or contact details, we may need information from you, such as the approximate time of a crash, to find the data. To exercise your rights, contact us at the address below. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features. We will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will also let you know in the App or in its store listing.
